**Potential Risk Index (PRI) Assessment Report**

This report details the Potential Risk Index (PRI) assessment for various infrastructure impacts, derived from the Hazard Index (HI), Exposure Index (EI), and Vulnerability Index (VI). The PRI is calculated as HI x EI x VI, normalized to a 0-4 scale. A default EI of 3 is applied uniformly where economic asset valuation data is unavailable.

---

### High and Moderate Risks (PRI 2)

The highest PRI scores of 2 indicate significant risks to the infrastructure. These are primarily driven by **Landslides** and **Changing precipitation patterns**, specifically concerning track bed integrity.

*   **Landslides and Debris Flow:**
    *   **Drivers:** These risks are characterized by a moderate Hazard Index (HI: 2), reflecting the frequency or intensity of heavy rainfall events (measured by the number of days with heavy rain per year).
    *   **Exposure:** The Exposure Index (EI) is high (4), indicating significant exposure of the infrastructure to these hazards.
    *   **Vulnerability:** The Vulnerability Index (VI) is also high (5), signifying a substantial susceptibility to damage and disruption.
    *   **Consequences:** The impacts include increased Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) due to damages, increased Operational Expenditure (OPEX) for maintenance, and potential stoppages of operations, leading to revenue loss.

*   **Changing Precipitation Patterns (Track Bed Damage):**
    *   **Drivers:** A moderate Hazard Index (HI: 2) is associated with changing precipitation patterns, specifically the number of days with heavy rain, impacting the track bed (ballast).
    *   **Exposure:** The infrastructure exhibits high Exposure (EI: 4).
    *   **Vulnerability:** The Vulnerability Index (VI) is high (4), indicating a significant susceptibility to damage from these precipitation-induced issues.
    *   **Consequences:** This primarily translates to increased OPEX for maintenance.
