**Risk Drivers**

* **HI (Hazard Impact)**: This metric assesses the potential impact of a hazard on an asset or system. A higher HI value indicates a greater potential for damage or disruption.
	+ Landslides and debris flow have a high HI value due to their potential to cause significant damage and disruption to operations.
	+ Temperature variability has a low HI value as it is less likely to cause direct damage or disruption.
* **EI (Economic Impact)**: This metric assesses the economic impact of a hazard on an asset or system. A higher EI value indicates a greater financial risk.
	+ All risks have a high EI value due to the potential for significant economic losses from disruptions to operations and maintenance costs.
* **VI (Vulnerability Index)**: This metric assesses the vulnerability of an asset or system to a hazard. A higher VI value indicates a greater vulnerability.
	+ Landslides and debris flow have a high VI value due to their potential to cause significant damage and disruption to operations.

**Recommendations**

Based on the PRI assessment, the top priority risks are:

1. Stop of operations due to landslides and debris flow.
2. Stop of operations due to heavy rain.

These risks should be addressed through proactive measures such as infrastructure design and construction that can withstand extreme weather events, and emergency preparedness plans to minimize disruptions to operations.
