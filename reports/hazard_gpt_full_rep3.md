## Climate Hazard Assessment for Railway Infrastructure

The table indicates that the railway infrastructure is exposed to a mix of **acute** and **chronic** climate hazards, with the most severe risks concentrated in **heat wave**, **droughts**, and **water stress**. ### Highest-priority hazards

**Heat wave** is the most critical hazard identified, with a **Hazard Level: Extreme** and **Hazard Index: 5**. The impact model points to **track buckling and equipment failure due to extreme heat**, indicating a direct threat to both rail geometry and operational safety.

Similarly, **droughts** are rated **Hazard Level: Extreme** with a **Hazard Index: 5**. The stated impact model highlights **soil desiccation affecting embankment stability and vegetation**, which can undermine the supporting ground conditions and increase vulnerability along the rail corridor.

**Water stress** is also a major concern, assessed at **Hazard Level: Very High** with a **Hazard Index: 4**. The table links this to **reduced water availability for maintenance and cooling operations**, suggesting operational constraints that may affect routine upkeep and resilience management.

**Landslide** is likewise classified as **Hazard Level: Medium** with a **Hazard Index: 2**. Its impact model, **slope instability triggered by intense precipitation events**, suggests a localized but significant threat, especially where the railway passes through unstable terrain.
