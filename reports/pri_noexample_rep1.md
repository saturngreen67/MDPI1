# PRI Assessment Report

## Executive Summary

This report presents a Prioritized Risk Index (PRI) assessment for infrastructure, focusing on the potential impacts of climate change. The assessment identifies and quantifies risks based on Hazard Impact (HI), Economic Impact (EI), and Vulnerability Impact (VI). Risks are categorized and presented in descending order of their PRI score, highlighting the most critical areas requiring attention.

## Methodology

The PRI is calculated using the formula: PRI = HI x EI x VI. The resulting score is normalized to a scale of 0-4.

*   **Hazard Impact (HI):** Assesses the likelihood and severity of the hazard occurring.
*   **Economic Impact (EI):** Assesses the financial consequences of the hazard. A default value of 3 is used uniformly due to the general unavailability of specific economic asset valuations in this dataset.
*   **Vulnerability Impact (VI):** Assesses the susceptibility of the infrastructure to the hazard.

## Conclusion

The PRI assessment highlights that risks associated with **landslides and debris flow**, driven by heavy rainfall, present the most significant threats, particularly concerning CAPEX, OPEX, and operational continuity. The uniform application of EI=4 underscores the potential financial implications across all assessed risks.
