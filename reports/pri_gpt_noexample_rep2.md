# PRI Assessment Report

## Scope and method

This report assesses the Physical Risk Index (PRI) for the listed climate-related impacts using the provided data table only. PRI is derived from **HI × EI × VI**, normalised to a **0–4 scale**. Where no economic asset valuation is provided, **EI = 3** is the conservative default; however, in the supplied table EI is consistently **4** across all entries, and that value is used as given.

A dash in HI would indicate no relevant hazard variation and would be treated as not applicable. No such dash appears in the table.

## Key drivers

Across the table, the dominant risk driver is **heavy-rain frequency**, which appears repeatedly as the climate indicator for:

This indicates that **precipitation-related hazards** are the principal source of physical risk in the dataset. Temperature and wind appear as secondary drivers with lower PRI values.

Based on the supplied data, the **highest PRI risks are landslide- and debris-flow-related impacts**, particularly where **VI is 5**.
