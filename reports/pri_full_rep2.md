**Potential Risk Index (PRI) Assessment Report**

This report details the Potential Risk Index (PRI) assessment for various infrastructure impacts, derived from the Hazard Index (HI), Exposure Index (EI), and Vulnerability Index (VI). The PRI is calculated as HI x EI x VI, normalized to a 0-4 scale. Where economic asset valuation was unavailable, the Exposure Index (EI) was conservatively set to 3.

**High and Moderate Risks (PRI 2)**

The highest PRI scores of 2 are associated with impacts driven by **Landslides** and **Changing Precipitation Patterns** affecting the track bed.

*   **Landslides and Debris Flow:**
    *   **Impacts:** These drivers contribute to **Reactive CAPEX** due to damages and **Increased Maintenance OPEX**, as well as **Operational Stops** leading to revenue loss.
    *   **Drivers:** The Hazard Index (HI) for landslides is rated at 2, indicating a moderate hazard level. This is compounded by a high Exposure Index (EI) of 4 and a very high Vulnerability Index (VI) of 5. The VI of 5 reflects the significant susceptibility of the infrastructure to damage from landslides and debris flow, particularly when triggered by a high number of days with heavy rain (HI: 2).
    *   **PRI Calculation:** For Reactive CAPEX and Increased Maintenance due to landslides, the PRI is calculated as 2 (HI) x 4 (EI) x 5 (VI) = 40, normalized to a PRI of 2. For Operational Stops due to landslides, the PRI is calculated as 2 (HI) x 4 (EI) x 5 (VI) = 40, normalized to a PRI of 2.

*   **Changing Precipitation Patterns (Track Bed Damage):**
    *   **Impact:** This driver leads to **Increased Maintenance OPEX** specifically for damage to the track bed (ballast).
    *   **Drivers:** The Hazard Index (HI) is rated at 2, indicating a moderate hazard associated with changes in precipitation types (rain, hail, snow/ice) and frequency of heavy rain days. The Exposure Index (EI) is high at 4, and the Vulnerability Index (VI) is also high at 4, signifying a substantial susceptibility of the track bed to damage under these conditions.
    *   **PRI Calculation:** The PRI is calculated as 2 (HI) x 4 (EI) x 4 (VI) = 32, normalized to a PRI of 2.

**Low Risks (PRI 1)**

A PRI of 1 is observed for impacts related to **Temperature Variability**, **Heavy Precipitation (Operational Stops)**, and **Changing Wind Patterns (Operational Stops)**.

*   **Temperature Variability:**
    *   **Impact:** This driver results in **Increased Maintenance OPEX** due to temperature increases.
    *   **Drivers:** The Hazard Index (HI) is low at 1, reflecting a minor hazard from temperature variability. The Exposure Index (EI) is high at 4, and the Vulnerability Index (VI) is moderate at 3.
    *   **PRI Calculation:** The PRI is calculated as 1 (HI) x 4 (EI) x 3 (VI) = 12, normalized to a PRI of 1.

*   **Heavy Precipitation (Operational Stops):**
    *   **Impact:** This driver leads to **Operational Stops** and associated revenue loss.
    *   **Drivers:** The Hazard Index (HI) is moderate at 2, linked to heavy precipitation events (rain, hail, snow/ice). The Exposure Index (EI) is high at 4, and the Vulnerability Index (VI) is moderate at 3, indicating a moderate susceptibility to operational disruption.
    *   **PRI Calculation:** The PRI is calculated as 2 (HI) x 4 (EI) x 3 (VI) = 24, normalized to a PRI of 1.

*   **Changing Wind Patterns (Operational Stops):**
    *   **Impact:** This driver causes **Operational Stops** and revenue loss.
    *   **Drivers:** The Hazard Index (HI) is low at 1, reflecting a minor hazard from changing wind patterns. The Exposure Index (EI) is high at 4, and the Vulnerability Index (VI) is low at 2, indicating low susceptibility to operational disruption from wind.
    *   **PRI Calculation:** The PRI is calculated as 1 (HI) x 4 (EI) x 2 (VI) = 8, normalized to a PRI of 1.

**No Risk (PRI 0)**

A PRI of 0 is assigned to impacts related to **Changing Precipitation Patterns (Increased Maintenance OPEX)**.

*   **Changing Precipitation Patterns (Increased Maintenance OPEX):**
    *   **Impact:** This driver leads to **Increased Maintenance OPEX**.
    *   **Drivers:** The Hazard Index (HI) is rated at 0, indicating no significant hazard from changes in precipitation patterns (rain, hail, snow/ice) as measured by mean annual rainfall. The Exposure Index (EI) is high at 4, and the Vulnerability Index (VI) is moderate at 3.
    *   **PRI Calculation:** The PRI is calculated as 0 (HI) x 4 (EI) x 3 (VI) = 0, normalized to a PRI of 0.
