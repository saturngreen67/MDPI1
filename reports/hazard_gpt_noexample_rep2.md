# Climate Hazard Assessment for Railway Infrastructure

## Overview

The railway infrastructure shows a mixed climate hazard profile, with several drivers presenting **medium to extreme** risks, while others are **low, no variation, or not applicable**. The most significant hazards are associated with **heat wave** and **droughts**, both rated **Hazard Level: Extreme** with **Hazard Index: 5**, indicating the highest concern in the dataset. In contrast, **changing precipitation patterns** show **Hazard Level: No variation** with **Hazard Index: 0**, and several drivers are marked **Not applicable**, indicating no relevant hazard variation for those drivers.

## Thermal and Temperature-Related Hazards

Temperature-related stress is an important issue for the railway system.

- **Changing temperature (air)** is assessed as **Hazard Level: Medium** with **Hazard Index: 2**. The stated impact model is **thermal expansion and structural stress on rail components over time**, indicating a persistent but moderate risk.
- **Temperature variability** is also rated **Hazard Level: Medium** with **Hazard Index: 2**. The impact model describes **cumulative thermal fatigue on rail and overhead infrastructure**, again suggesting a moderate long-term stressor.
- **Heat wave** represents the most severe thermal hazard, with **Hazard Level: Extreme** and **Hazard Index: 5**. The impact model highlights **track buckling and equipment failure due to extreme heat**, making this a critical operational and structural concern.
- **Cold waves/frost** are marked **Hazard Level: Not applicable** with **Hazard Index: -**, meaning no relevant hazard variation is identified for this driver.

## Water and Moisture-Related Hazards

Water-related conditions present both direct and indirect risks to railway operations.

- **Water stress** is rated **Hazard Level: Very High** with **Hazard Index: 4**. The impact model notes **reduced water availability for maintenance and cooling operations**, indicating substantial operational strain.
- **Droughts** are assessed as **Hazard Level: Extreme** with **Hazard Index: 5**. The impact model identifies **soil desiccation affecting embankment stability and vegetation**, which suggests severe infrastructure and maintenance implications.
- **Heavy precipitation (rain, hail, snow/ice)** is rated **Hazard Level: Medium** with **Hazard Index: 2**. The impact model points to **flash flooding and drainage overload affecting track operations**, showing a moderate but meaningful disruption risk.
- **Changing precipitation patterns** are classified as **Hazard Level: No variation** with **Hazard Index: 0**. The impact model states **limited change in total precipitation; minimal direct operational impact**, indicating no meaningful hazard variation.
- **Storm (winds)** is marked **Hazard Level: Not applicable** with **Hazard Index: -**, so no relevant hazard variation is identified for this driver.

## Wind and Storm-Related Hazards

Wind-related effects are generally limited in this dataset, except where tied to broader storm conditions.

- **Changing wind patterns** are rated **Hazard Level: Low** with **Hazard Index: 1**. The impact model describes **minor crosswind effects on train stability**, suggesting limited concern.
- **Storm (winds)** is **Not applicable** with **Hazard Index: -**, meaning no relevant hazard variation is recorded for this driver.

## Geotechnical and Slope Instability Hazards

Slope and ground stability issues are present and linked to weather extremes.

- **Landslide** is rated **Hazard Level: Medium** with **Hazard Index: 2**. The impact model identifies **slope instability triggered by intense precipitation events**, indicating a moderate hazard that can affect railway continuity and safety.

## Fire-Related Hazards

- **Wildfires** are marked **Hazard Level: Not applicable** with **Hazard Index: -**, indicating no relevant hazard variation for this driver in the provided table.

## Key Takeaways

The railway hazard profile is dominated by:

- **Extreme risks** from **Heat wave** and **Droughts**, both with **Hazard Index: 5**
- **Very High risk** from **Water stress** with **Hazard Index: 4**
- Several **Medium** hazards tied to temperature change, precipitation extremes, and landslide potential
- Limited exposure to wind-related change, and **no variation / not applicable** classifications for some drivers, including **changing precipitation patterns**, **wildfires**, **cold waves/frost**, and **storm (winds)**

Overall, the table indicates that the railway system is most vulnerable to **heat, drought, and water-related stress**, with additional moderate risks from **thermal fatigue, heavy precipitation, and slope instability**.
