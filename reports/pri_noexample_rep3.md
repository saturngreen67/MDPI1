# PRI Assessment Report

This report details the Infrastructure Risk Assessment (PRI) for identified climate-related hazards. The PRI is calculated using the formula: PRI = HI x EI x VI, normalised to a 0-4 scale. Hazard Impact (HI) reflects the susceptibility of the infrastructure to the hazard. Economic Impact (EI) represents the financial consequences, with a default value of 3 applied uniformly due to the absence of specific economic asset valuations. Vulnerability Impact (VI) quantifies the sensitivity and adaptive capacity of the infrastructure to the hazard.

## Highest Priority Risks

### 1. Landslides and Debris Flow Impacts

**Description:** Risks associated with landslides and debris flow, impacting both Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) and Operational Expenditure (OPEX), as well as operational continuity.

*   **Reactive CAPEX due to damages associated to landslides and debris flow.**
    *   **Hazard:** Landslides
    *   **Climate Indicator:** Number of days with heavy rain (days/year)
    *   **HI:** 2 (Moderate susceptibility to landslides triggered by heavy rainfall).
    *   **EI:** 4 (Significant economic impact, assuming high asset value and cost of repair/rebuilding).
    *   **VI:** 5 (Very high vulnerability, indicating limited adaptive capacity or high sensitivity to landslide events).
    *   **PRI:** 2 (HI 2 x EI 4 x VI 5 = 40, normalised to 2).
    *   **Explanation:** This risk is driven by the moderate susceptibility of the infrastructure to landslides (HI=2), amplified by a significant economic impact (EI=4) and a very high vulnerability to such events (VI=5). The primary climate driver is the increasing number of days with heavy rainfall, which directly correlates with landslide triggers.

*   **Increased maintenance due to landslides and debris flow.**
    *   **Hazard:** Landslides
    *   **Climate Indicator:** Number of days with heavy rain (days/year)
    *   **HI:** 2 (Moderate susceptibility to landslides triggering maintenance needs).
    *   **EI:** 4 (Significant economic impact, assuming high maintenance costs).
    *   **VI:** 5 (Very high vulnerability, indicating high sensitivity to landslide-induced maintenance requirements).
    *   **PRI:** 2 (HI 2 x EI 4 x VI 5 = 40, normalised to 2).
    *   **Explanation:** Similar to the CAPEX risk, this maintenance-related risk stems from moderate landslide susceptibility (HI=2), a significant economic impact (EI=4), and very high vulnerability (VI=5). Heavy rainfall days are the key climate indicator.

*   **Stop of operations due to landslides and debris flow.**
    *   **Hazard:** Landslides
    *   **Climate Indicator:** Number of days with heavy rain (days/year)
    *   **HI:** 2 (Moderate susceptibility to landslides causing operational disruptions).
    *   **EI:** 4 (Significant economic impact due to lost revenue).
    *   **VI:** 5 (Very high vulnerability, indicating limited resilience to landslide-induced operational stoppages).
    *   **PRI:** 2 (HI 2 x EI 4 x VI 5 = 40, normalised to 2).
    *   **Explanation:** This operational risk is driven by moderate landslide susceptibility (HI=2), significant economic consequences from revenue loss (EI=4), and very high vulnerability (VI=5). The number of heavy rainfall days is the critical climate driver.
