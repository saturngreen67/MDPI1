## Climate Hazard Analysis Report: Railway Infrastructure

This report analyzes the climate-related hazards impacting railway infrastructure based on the provided Hazard Data Table. The analysis categorizes hazards into thermal, hydrological, and other related impacts, detailing their respective Hazard Levels and Indices.

### Thermal Hazards

The railway infrastructure is exposed to significant thermal hazards. **Heat waves** present an **Extreme** hazard (Index 5) due to the risk of track buckling and equipment failure. **Changing temperature (air)** also poses a **Medium** hazard (Index 2), driven by thermal expansion and structural stress on rail components over time. Similarly, **Temperature variability** results in **Medium** hazard (Index 2) from cumulative thermal fatigue affecting both rail and overhead infrastructure. Conversely, **Cold waves/frost** are noted as **Not applicable** (Index -) in terms of relevant hazard variation.

### Hydrological Hazards

Hydrological factors present substantial risks to railway operations. **Droughts** represent an **Extreme** hazard (Index 5), primarily due to soil desiccation impacting embankment stability and vegetation. **Water stress** is identified as a **Very High** hazard (Index 4), stemming from reduced water availability crucial for maintenance and cooling operations. **Heavy precipitation (rain, hail, snow/ice)** poses a **Medium** hazard (Index 2), with risks of flash flooding and drainage overload affecting track operations. **Landslide** is also classified as a **Medium** hazard (Index 2), typically triggered by intense precipitation events leading to slope instability. **Changing precipitation patterns** are indicated to have **No variation** (Index 0) in terms of total precipitation and minimal direct operational impact.

### Other Climate-Related Hazards

Other climate drivers also contribute to potential risks. **Changing wind patterns** result in a **Low** hazard (Index 1) due to minor crosswind effects on train stability. **Wildfires** and **Storms (winds)** are listed as **Not applicable** (Index -) for hazard variation within this dataset.
