**Risk Drivers**

* **Hazard Impact (HI)**: HI scores range from 0 to 5. A score of 0 indicates no relevant hazard variation, while a score of 5 indicates high impact.
	+ Landslides and debris flow have a HI of 2, indicating moderate impact on infrastructure.
	+ Changing precipitation patterns and types have a HI of 0 or 2, indicating low to moderate impact.
	+ Heavy precipitation has a HI of 2, indicating moderate impact.
* **Economic Impact (EI)**: EI scores range from 1 to 4. A score of 3 is applied as a conservative default due to data unavailability. This indicates that the economic impact is significant but not quantified.
* **Vulnerability Index (VI)**: VI scores range from 2 to 5, with higher values indicating higher vulnerability.

**Recommendations**

Based on the PRI assessment, it is recommended that infrastructure managers prioritize mitigation and adaptation strategies for landslides and debris flow, changing precipitation patterns, and heavy precipitation. This includes:

* Conducting thorough risk assessments and developing emergency response plans.
* Implementing engineering measures to reduce vulnerability to landslides and debris flow.
* Developing early warning systems for heavy precipitation events.

**Limitations**

The PRI assessment is based on available data and may not capture all relevant climate-related hazards. Further research and data collection are necessary to improve the accuracy of the assessment.
