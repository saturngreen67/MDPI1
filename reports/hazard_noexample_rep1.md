## Climate Hazard Analysis Report: Railway Infrastructure

This report analyzes the climate-related hazards impacting railway infrastructure, based on the provided Hazard Data Table. The analysis groups hazards by their nature and assesses their severity using the Hazard Index and Hazard Level.

### Thermal Hazards

Railway infrastructure is exposed to significant risks from changing temperature patterns. **Heat waves** present the most severe threat, with a **Hazard Index of 5** and an **Extreme Hazard Level**. This is attributed to track buckling and equipment failure caused by extreme heat. **Changing temperature (air)** also poses a **Medium** risk (**Hazard Index 2**) due to thermal expansion and structural stress on rail components over time. Furthermore, **temperature variability** contributes to **Medium** risk (**Hazard Index 2**) through cumulative thermal fatigue on rail and overhead infrastructure.

Conversely, **cold waves/frost** are noted as **Not applicable**, indicating no relevant hazard variation for this driver.

### Hydrological Hazards

Water-related hazards present a mixed picture for railway operations. **Droughts** are identified as a critical risk, with a **Hazard Index of 5** and an **Extreme Hazard Level**, primarily due to soil desiccation affecting embankment stability and vegetation. **Water stress** also represents a **Very High** risk (**Hazard Index 4**) due to reduced water availability for essential maintenance and cooling operations.

**Heavy precipitation (rain, hail, snow/ice)** poses a **Medium** threat (**Hazard Index 2**), leading to flash flooding and drainage overload that can affect track operations. **Landslides**, triggered by intense precipitation events, also represent a **Medium** risk (**Hazard Index 2**) due to slope instability. **Changing precipitation patterns** are assessed as having **No variation** (**Hazard Index 0**) in total precipitation and minimal direct operational impact.

### Other Hazards

**Changing wind patterns** are associated with a **Low** risk (**Hazard Index 1**) due to minor crosswind effects on train stability. **Storms (winds)** are categorized as **Not applicable**, suggesting no significant variation in hazard for this driver. **Wildfires** are also classified as **Not applicable**, indicating no relevant hazard variation.
