## Climate Hazard Analysis Report: Railway Infrastructure

This report analyzes the climate-related hazards affecting railway infrastructure based on the provided Hazard Data Table. The analysis groups hazards by their nature and assesses their potential impact and severity.

### Temperature-Related Hazards

**Extreme Heat:** The most significant temperature-related hazard identified is **Heat wave**, with a **Hazard Index of 5** and a **Hazard Level of Extreme**. This is attributed to track buckling and equipment failure caused by extreme heat.

**Changing Temperatures and Variability:** **Changing temperature (air)** presents a **Medium** hazard (**Hazard Index 2**), primarily due to thermal expansion and structural stress on rail components over time. Similarly, **Temperature variability** also poses a **Medium** hazard (**Hazard Index 2**) through cumulative thermal fatigue on rail and overhead infrastructure.

**Cold Waves:** **Cold waves/frost** are classified as **Not applicable** for direct operational impact, indicated by a '-' in the Hazard Index.

### Water-Related Hazards

**Water Stress and Droughts:** **Water stress** is identified as a **Very High** hazard (**Hazard Index 4**), impacting reduced water availability for maintenance and cooling operations. **Droughts** represent an **Extreme** hazard (**Hazard Index 5**), leading to soil desiccation which affects embankment stability and vegetation.

**Precipitation Impacts:** **Heavy precipitation (rain, hail, snow/ice)** poses a **Medium** hazard (**Hazard Index 2**) due to flash flooding and drainage overload that can affect track operations. **Changing precipitation patterns** are noted to have **minimal direct operational impact**, resulting in a **Hazard Level of No variation** and a **Hazard Index of 0**.

### Other Hazards

**Landslides:** **Landslide** risks are classified as **Medium** (**Hazard Index 2**), specifically triggered by intense precipitation events leading to slope instability.

**Wind-Related Hazards:** **Changing wind patterns** are assessed as a **Low** hazard (**Hazard Index 1**) with minor crosswind effects on train stability. **Storm (winds)** events are classified as **Not applicable** for direct operational impact, indicated by a '-' in the Hazard Index.

**Fire Hazards:** **Wildfires** are classified as **Not applicable** for direct operational impact, indicated by a '-' in the Hazard Index.
